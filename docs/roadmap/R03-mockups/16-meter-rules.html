A reading that crosses a rule raises a condition ticket on EOT02, found by condition monitoring. The engineer decides the work on the ticket. While that ticket is open, a later crossing adds a line to it. Every reading is checked: the Readings sheet, the equipment page and PM jobs.
The limits 0.4–1.2 mm only mark a reading High or Low. They raise no ticket.
The model's rules are set in Masters › Models › Meters, and reach every crane of the model.
