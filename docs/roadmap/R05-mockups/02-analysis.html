Ticket's cause and root cause are different things. The ticket's cause says why this part failed this time. The root cause says why the plant let it happen. Fixing the root cause stops the next one.
Why-whyAdd why
1. Why did the press not start?
The control supply did not reach the start circuit. The main contactor's NC auxiliary contact did not pass it.
2. Why did the contact not pass the supply?
Its contact faces were coated with fine dust and grime. Evidence: seen when opened; supply came back after cleaning.
3. Why was there dust on the contacts?
Dust gets into the panel. The door gasket is torn and the cable entry at the bottom is open.
4. Why was it not cleaned before it failed?
No task cleans the panel or checks the contactors. Panels are cleaned only after a fault.
5. Why is there no such task?
The presses have no preventive maintenance plan yet.
Root cause 4.2 Management error ISO 14224 group: management
Dust enters the press panels, and nobody cleans the contactors before they fail.
